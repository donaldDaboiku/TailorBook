Privacy Notice for TailorMate

Last updated: 02/11/2026

This Privacy Notice explains how gitsystemng ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares personal data through the TailorMate application (the "App"). This notice applies to shop owners who use the App and the clients whose data is entered into the App by those shop owners.

1. Who we are

TailorMate is operated by gitsystemng. For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act, gitsystemng is the data controller for the personal data of shop owners. For the personal data of clients entered into the App, the shop is the data controller, and gitsystemng acts as a data processor on the shop's behalf.

2. Personal data we collect and process

a) Shop owner data. When you create a shop account, we collect and store the following information: name, email address, shop name, phone number, WhatsApp number, state, address, shop logo, and receipt message. Your password is stored in a secure, non-reversible encrypted format and cannot be read by us.

b) Client data. The App allows shops to record and manage information about their clients. This data is entered and controlled by the shop and includes: name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, gender, address, notes, body measurements, job details, and payment records. Catalogue photos uploaded to the App are of clothing items, not individuals.

c) Payment data. Subscription payments are processed by Paystack. We do not store your card number. We retain a record of the payment reference and amount for accounting and subscription management purposes.

3. How we use personal data

We use shop owner data to create and manage your shop account, provide access to the App's features, process subscription payments via Paystack, communicate with you about your account (including password resets and service notices), and comply with legal obligations.

Shops use client data to manage their business operations, including tailoring orders, measurements, and payment tracking.

4. Data sharing and third-party processors

We share personal data with the following third parties:

Paystack receives the shop's email address to process subscription payments. Paystack maintains its own records in accordance with its privacy policy.

Render hosts our database and file storage outside Nigeria. Render may retain backups of deleted data for its own backup period.

Emailed receipts and notices are sent through a third-party email service.

WhatsApp communications remain on the WhatsApp platform and are subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.

Admin access. A TailorMate administrator can view a shop's name, email, phone number, and state, and can set a new password for a shop account. Such password resets are logged with the reason. Administrators do not have access to client measurement data.

5. Data retention and deletion

A client's record remains in the App until the shop erases that client or deletes the shop account. Archiving a client only hides the record. It does not delete it.

Deleting a shop account permanently removes the login credentials, shop profile, client records, measurements, jobs, payments, expenses, logo, catalogue photos, and any feedback submitted from that login. A referral note that named the shop is rewritten to remove the shop's name.

Paystack retains its own payment records independently. Render may retain backups for its standard backup period following deletion.

6. Your rights

Shop owners can download a copy of all their records or delete the account at any time from the More section of the App.

Clients should contact the shop that holds their data to ask for a copy, a correction, or deletion. The shop can correct the details, download the file, or erase the record in the App.

7. Eligibility

You must be 18 years of age or older to open a shop account.

8. Data breach notification

If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours. Where the risk is high, we will also notify the affected shops without undue delay.

9. Contact us

For questions about this Privacy Notice or to exercise your rights, contact GITsystem. Website: gitsystemng.com. Email: info@gitsystemng.com. WhatsApp: +2348100197402.

You may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.
